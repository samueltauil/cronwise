Cronwise

Cron notation, explained plainly.

This demo project exists to make scheduled jobs easier to read and verify.

The five cron fields

minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week
  0      9        *          *       1-5
Field Example Meaning
minute0at minute zero
hour9at 9 AM
day/month*every day / every month
day/week1-5Monday through Friday

Simple examples

0 9 * * 1-5    # 9:00 AM on weekdays
*/15 * * * *   # every 15 minutes
30 2 1 * *     # 2:30 AM on the first of each month

Cronwise turns these compact expressions into plain language and shows the next scheduled runs.
